1GRAMMAR imperatives, object pronouns: me, him, etc.

a 7.10 Read and listen to a film director and some actors. Does Scarlett love Sam? Does she love Rupert?

Director OK. 'Be quiet, please. Scene one, take one. Lights, camera,... action!'

Scarlett Hello, Sam.

Sam Hello, Scarlett.

Scarlett in. Sit down. We need to talk.

Sam Talk? What about?

Scarlett Us. You and me.

Sam to me, Scarlett. I love you! Do you love me?

Scarlett I don't know, Sam. I need time.

Sam And Rupert? Do you love him?

Scarlett Don't about Rupert. Yes, I like Rupert, but I don't love him. You don't understand.

Sam cry, Scarlett. Please don't cry.

Director Cut! Great! I like it.

Director OK, Scene two. This is a big scene. Scarlett, to the windows. them. Sam, go to Scarlett. next to her. Scarlett, at him. That's great. Don't move.

Sam What do I say to her? I don't remember.

Director Nothing. Don't anything. Kiss her.
